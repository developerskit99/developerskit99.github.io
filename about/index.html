About DevelopersKit

We built DevelopersKit because finding reliable, fast, and private online tools shouldn't be hard. Most tool websites are cluttered with ads, require sign-ups, or upload your data to unknown servers. We wanted something different.

What We Offer

DevelopersKit is a collection of 230 free browser-based tools spanning 10 categories: Developer Tools, CSS/HTML Tools, Text Tools, Math Calculators, Student Tools, Date & Time, Finance Calculators, Business Calculators, Color Tools, and Image Tools.

Whether you need to format JSON, calculate your EMI, count words, resize an image, or pick a color palette — we have a tool for it. Every tool is designed to be fast, accurate, and easy to use.

How It Works

All tools run entirely in your browser using modern web APIs and JavaScript. When you paste text into the JSON Formatter or upload an image to the Resizer, the processing happens on your device. Your data never leaves your browser. There are no server uploads and no databases storing your input. We use anonymous page-view analytics (Google Analytics) to understand overall traffic; this never captures what you type, paste, or upload into the tools.

Our Principles

Privacy First

Your data stays on your device. We cannot see what you enter or process. No uploads. No account required. Processing happens locally in your browser.

Always Free

No sign-ups, no premium tiers, no hidden costs. Every tool is free to use, today and tomorrow.

Fast & Lightweight

No heavy frameworks or bloated libraries. Pages load fast and tools respond instantly.

Works Offline

Once loaded, most tools work without an internet connection. Use them anywhere, anytime.

Accessible

Built with keyboard navigation, screen reader support, and WCAG-compliant contrast ratios.

No Account Required

Jump in and use any tool immediately. No email, no password, no registration form.

Built With

DevelopersKit is built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no build tools, no dependencies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and the source code is open for anyone to inspect.

FAQ

What is DevelopersKit?

DevelopersKit is a free collection of 230 browser-based tools for developers, students, and professionals. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — no data is uploaded to any server.

Who built DevelopersKit?

DevelopersKit was created by a web developer passionate about building fast, accessible, and privacy-respecting tools that help people get things done without complicated software.
