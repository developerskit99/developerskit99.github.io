# SERP research (observed 2026-09-18, desktop, India locale)

## age calculator
- Type: tool intent; Google calculator widget expected; organic top 10: calculator.net, whatsmyage.in, calculatestuff.com, calculatorsoup.com, omnicalculator.com, economictimes.indiatimes.com + apps/med/edu domains.
- Read: entrenched calculator brands own head term. Long-tail + question intent is the realistic path.

## json formatter online
- Type: pure tool-vs-tool SERP, no domain giants. jsonformatter.org, jsoneditoronline.org, json.site, curiousconcept, json-indent.com, reqbin, jam.dev, jsonbeautifier.org, static.app.
- Read: several small independents rank. Privacy angle is a live differentiator in titles ("Private, Secure & Free", "100% In-Browser", "Free, Open Source & Ad-free") — matches DevelopersKit positioning. Best head-term odds in the set.

## percentage calculator
- Type: Google widget + calculator.net/soup/omni; but also weak domains (school sites, moovago.com, phoenixgreens.com) in top 10.
- Read: soft spots exist, but widget absorbs most clicks. Long-tail first.

## word counter
- Type: brand-dominated: wordcounter.net, quillbot.com, grammarly.com, wordcounter.io, zerogpt.com, gotranscript.com, word.cloud.microsoft.
- Read: unwinnable head term short-term. Long-tail only.

## emi calculator home loan (India locale)
- Type: top 10 ALL Indian banks (HDFC, SBI, Axis, BoB, ICICI) + SEBI investor portal + groww.in.
- Read: completely owned by finance institutions. Never chase head term; prepayment/tenure/amount long-tail only.

## how to calculate age from date of birth (question)
