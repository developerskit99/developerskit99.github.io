Loan Prepayment Guide

Prepaying a home or personal loan can save lakhs in interest. Learn how part-prepayments shorten tenure, then model your own loan with our free EMI calculator.

How prepayment works

Every EMI has two parts: interest and principal. Early in the loan, most of your EMI pays interest. A part-prepayment goes directly against principal, so all future EMIs accrue less interest.

Tenure vs EMI reduction

After a prepayment, lenders let you keep the EMI and shorten the tenure, or keep the tenure and lower the EMI. Shortening tenure normally saves far more total interest.

Worked example

On a 20-year home loan, a single 10% prepayment in year 3 can shave off more than a year of tenure and save several EMIs worth of interest. Enter your own numbers in the EMI calculator linked below to see your exact saving.

FAQ

Does prepayment always save money?

Almost always, when the prepayment goes fully toward principal. Compare the interest saved against any prepayment penalty your lender charges.

Should I reduce tenure or EMI after prepaying?

Reducing tenure usually saves more interest because the loan closes earlier. Reducing EMI helps monthly cash flow instead.

Is there a penalty for prepaying a loan?

Many floating-rate home loans have no prepayment penalty, but fixed-rate loans and personal loans often do. Check your loan agreement first.
