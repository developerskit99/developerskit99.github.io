Simple vs compound

Simple interest pays only on principal. Compound interest pays on principal plus past interest - the snowball that makes long horizons powerful.

Frequency matters

The same 8% grows faster compounded monthly than annually. Banks quote nominal rates; always compare effective annual rates.

The Rule of 72

The fastest mental estimator in finance: years to double = 72 / rate. Verify any estimate instantly with the calculator below.

FAQ

What is the compound interest formula?

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt): principal times one plus the per-period rate, raised to the number of periods.

What is the Rule of 72?

Divide 72 by the annual rate to estimate doubling time. At 8%, money doubles in about 9 years.

Monthly vs annual compounding?

More frequent compounding earns slightly more: monthly beats quarterly beats annual, all else equal.

Is this financial advice?

No. Educational projections only; consult a qualified advisor for decisions.
