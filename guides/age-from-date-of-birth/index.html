The exact method

Age = today minus birth date, computed in years, then remaining months, then remaining days - with borrowing when the day or month goes negative.

Leap years and edge cases

Feb 29 birthdays, month-end dates (Jan 31 to Feb 28), and timezone boundaries are where naive calculators go wrong. This tool handles all three.

FAQ

What is the formula to calculate age?

Subtract the birth date from today's date, borrowing months and days like ordinary subtraction. If this month's birthday has not occurred yet, subtract one year.

How to calculate age in dd mm yyyy?

Enter the date of birth in day-month-year order into the calculator above; it parses dd-mm-yyyy as well as yyyy-mm-dd formats.

Do leap years affect age calculation?

Yes, and the calculator accounts for them: someone born on Feb 29 legally ages on Feb 28 or Mar 1 in non-leap years depending on jurisdiction.

Is my birth date stored?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and your date of birth is never sent anywhere.
