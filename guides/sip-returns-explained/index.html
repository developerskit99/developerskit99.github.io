SIP Returns Explained

SIP returns confuse even seasoned investors. Learn how monthly SIPs compound, what XIRR means, and how to project your own SIP growth with a free calculator.

How SIP math works

Each monthly installment compounds separately for its remaining duration. The future value is the sum of 12+ individually grown amounts per year.

XIRR in plain words

XIRR is the single annual rate that explains your SIP's actual growth, accounting for the timing of every installment. It is the only fair way to compare SIPs.

Worked example

10,000 per month for 10 years at 12% grows to roughly 23 lakh on 12 lakh invested. Change any input in the calculator to model your own plan.

FAQ

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan invests a fixed amount every month into a mutual fund. Returns compound because each installment grows for a different length of time.

What returns can I expect from a SIP?

Equity SIPs in India have historically returned roughly 10-14% annually over long periods, but returns are market-linked and never guaranteed.

SIP vs lump sum: which is better?

Lump sum wins in steadily rising markets; SIPs win on discipline and rupee-cost averaging in volatile ones. Neither is guaranteed.
