The three core calculations

Almost every percentage problem is one of three types: find X% of N, find what percent A is of B, or find the whole when a part and its percent are known.

Worked examples

Discount: 25% off 80 = 20 off, pay 60. Test score: 42/50 = 84%. Tip: 15% of a 1,200 bill = 180.

Percentages in daily life

GST and VAT, salary hikes, loan interest, exam scores, and battery levels are all the same three calculations wearing different clothes.

FAQ

How do I find X% of a number?

Multiply the number by X and divide by 100. 15% of 200 = (200 x 15) / 100 = 30.

How do I find what percent A is of B?

Divide A by B and multiply by 100. 30 out of 50 = (30/50) x 100 = 60%.

How do I reverse a percentage?

If 30 is 15% of some number, divide: 30 / 0.15 = 200. The original number was 200.
