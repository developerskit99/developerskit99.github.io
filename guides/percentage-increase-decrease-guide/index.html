Percentage Increase vs Decrease

Price hikes, salary raises, and growth rates are all percentage increases. Learn the formula with worked examples, then calculate any increase with our free tool.

The formula

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, multiply by 100. That is the entire method.

Worked examples

Salary 40,000 to 44,000: (4,000/40,000) x 100 = 10% raise. Price 200 to 150 is not an increase at all - that is a 25% decrease, covered by the companion calculator.

Common mistakes

Using the new value as the denominator, and confusing percentage points with percent. A rise from 10% to 12% is 2 percentage points but a 20% increase.

FAQ

What is the percentage increase formula?

Increase = ((New - Old) / Old) x 100. For example, 50 to 75 is ((75-50)/50) x 100 = 50%.

What is the difference between increase and decrease?

Increase measures growth from a smaller old value; decrease measures a drop. Each has its own calculator linked below.

Can increase exceed 100%?

Yes. Doubling is a 100% increase; tripling is 200%. There is no upper limit.
