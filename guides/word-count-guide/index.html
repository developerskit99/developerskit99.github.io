Word Count Guide

Word counts decide grades, publishing fees and SEO limits. Learn how counting works, why tools disagree, and how to get an exact count free and privately.

Counting rules

Words = whitespace-separated tokens. Characters = every symbol including spaces (or excluding them). Sentences = segments ending in . ! ?. Reading time assumes ~200 words per minute.

Why counts differ

Is 'well-known' one word or two? Is '2026' a word? Every tool answers slightly differently; consistency within one tool matters more than the absolute number.

Hitting exact limits

Write 10% under the limit, then trim. Paste the final text into the counter for the exact number before submitting essays, applications, or meta descriptions.

FAQ

How does a word counter work?

It splits text on whitespace. Hyphenated words, numbers, and contractions each count per the tool's rules, which is why tools differ slightly.

Characters with or without spaces?

Both matter: social platforms usually count with spaces; SMS segments and some forms count without. This tool shows both.

Why do two counters disagree?

Different rules for hyphens, slashes, em-dashes, and numbers. For submissions, always use the recipient's own counter as final.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Counting runs locally in your browser; your text never leaves your device.
